    porchlight
    A light left on for every neighbour.
    Hack the Change 2026 · Calgary

    Someone always checks on the people who live alone.

    Porchlight is a box by the door and the people behind it: nurses, home-care workers, family and neighbours. This demo plays back a real night, January 13, 2024, when Calgary hit −30 and Alberta's grid nearly ran out.

    1. The box on the left is Margaret's home: I'm OK and Help.
    2. The map is Calgary. Click any home to see how they are, or ask for a check-in.
    3. The phone on the right is a caregiver's. When someone doesn't answer, their phone gets asked.

    The bar under the clock always tells you what's happening and what to try next.
