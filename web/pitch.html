Why it works

Real data, simple rules, people already on the street.

  • Grid data from AESO, temperatures from Environment Canada, and the official report on January 13. Nothing made up.
  • Rules a person can read: the AI only writes the words, never decides who to ask.
  • No camera, no microphone. The AI never sees an address or a phone number.
  • Nothing to log into at home; bigger text, high contrast and four languages for the people who respond.
  • About US$59 a box and US$5 a month for a whole community, run by the 150+ community associations Calgary already has.
